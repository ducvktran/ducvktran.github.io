About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at National University of Singapore, working in the NUS Ubicomp Lab under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Brian Y. Lim. Before this, I studied Mathematics and Linguistics at the University of Texas at Austin. My current research areas include explainable AI (XAI) and human-computer interaction (HCI), with main focus on AI-assisted decision support tools.
